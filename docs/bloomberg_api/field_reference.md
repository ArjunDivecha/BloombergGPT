# Field Reference Guide

## Equity Fields
- PX_LAST: Last traded price
- PX_OPEN: Opening price
- PX_HIGH: High price
- PX_LOW: Low price
- PX_VOLUME: Trading volume
- MARKET_CAP: Market capitalization
- DVD_EX_DT: Dividend ex-date
- DVD_SH_DT: Dividend pay date
- CHG_PCT_1D: 1-day price change
- CHG_PCT_1M: 1-month price change
- PE_RATIO: Price to earnings ratio

## Index Fields
- INDX_MWEIGHT: Index members
- INDX_MWEIGHT_PCT: Member weights
- INDX_LEVEL: Index level

## Currency Fields
- EURUSD Curncy: EUR/USD exchange rate
- GBPUSD Curncy: GBP/USD exchange rate
- JPYUSD Curncy: JPY/USD exchange rate

## Commodity Fields
- CL1 Comdty: WTI Crude Oil
- GC1 Comdty: Gold
- SI1 Comdty: Silver

## Field Descriptions
- PX_LAST: The most recent trade price
- DVD_EX_DT: Date when stock trades without dividend
- MARKET_CAP: Total market value of outstanding shares
- VOLUME: Number of shares traded

## Field Categories
- Pricing: PX_LAST, PX_OPEN, PX_HIGH, PX_LOW
- Dividends: DVD_EX_DT, DVD_SH_DT, DVD_RATE
- Ratios: PE_RATIO, PB_RATIO
- Volume: PX_VOLUME, AVG_VOLUME
