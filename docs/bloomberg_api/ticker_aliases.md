# Ticker Alias Guide

## Equity Tickers
- AAPL US Equity: Apple Inc.
- MSFT US Equity: Microsoft Corporation
- GOOGL US Equity: Alphabet Inc.
- AMZN US Equity: Amazon.com Inc.
- TSLA US Equity: Tesla Inc.

## Index Tickers
- SPX Index: S&P 500 Index
- INDU Index: Dow Jones Industrial Average
- VIX Index: CBOE Volatility Index

## Currency Tickers
- EURUSD Curncy: Euro vs US Dollar
- GBPUSD Curncy: British Pound vs US Dollar
- USDJPY Curncy: US Dollar vs Japanese Yen

## Commodity Tickers
- CL1 Comdty: WTI Crude Oil Generic 1st
- GC1 Comdty: Gold Spot
- SI1 Comdty: Silver Spot

## Government Bonds
- USGG10YR Index: US Generic 10 Year
- USGG30YR Index: US Generic 30 Year

## Ticker Formats
- US Equity: US stocks
- Index: Market indices
- Curncy: Currency pairs
- Comdty: Commodities
- Govt: Government bonds

## Regional Variations
- UK equities: LON suffix
- European equities: vary by exchange
- Asian equities: vary by exchange

## Confidence Levels
- High: Well-known tickers like AAPL
- Medium: Common aliases
- Low: Regional or less common tickers
